# Security+ study plan

Target: **SY0-701 / V7**. Verify your booked exam code before beginning. Adjust the pace to your starting knowledge and the evidence in your study tracker.

## Your 45-minute study block

| Time | Activity | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 minutes | Recall yesterday without notes | One drawing and three explanations |
| 15 minutes | Read and reason through a lesson | Explain the decision and one trade-off |
| 15 minutes | Complete a drill, PBQ-style task or lab | Your answer or observed result |
| 10 minutes | Review missed or guessed answers | Corrected reasoning linked to an objective |

## Eight-week roadmap

| Weeks | Focus | Checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | General concepts; threats, vulnerabilities and mitigations | Explain control categories, trust, cryptography and attack patterns |
| 3–4 | Security architecture; operations foundations | Draw trust boundaries and complete the hash and policy labs |
| 5–6 | Remaining operations; governance, risk and oversight | Correlate evidence, calculate risk and explain recovery targets |
| 7–8 | PBQ-style exercises; mixed questions; weak objectives | Explain mistakes, repeat difficult tasks and verify exam version |

Use all detailed official objective bullets to identify gaps. A good practice-set score is only one signal; this collection does not provide a validated exam-readiness threshold.

## Build one portfolio project

Choose an incident case file, recovery proof or control portfolio from the handbook's final page. Document:

1. Your goal, scope and assumptions.
2. A diagram and expected behavior.
3. Your procedure and observed evidence.
4. One failure, your reasoning and the repair.
5. Positive and negative checks, remaining limitations and next steps.

Use invented names, synthetic logs and disposable lab data in public examples.
